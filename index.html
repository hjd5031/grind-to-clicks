How is this calculated?

We use published measurements of ground coffee at known click settings. We average neighboring measurements where the trend reverses, then interpolate within the supported range and round to the nearest click. Results are starting points and can vary with the beans and grinder zero point.

Check the statistic next to your result. Onyx labels its values average particle size. The K6 source reports the median (D50). These statistics can differ. For Red Clix, we apply Comandante’s 2:1 click ratio to the C40 reference. Models without enough measurements link to their manufacturer’s setting guide.
